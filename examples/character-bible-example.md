# Character Bible Example

## Character: Priya (for a luxury skincare campaign)

**Full locked description (use in every prompt):**

South Asian woman, mid 30s, warm medium brown skin, sharp jaw, deep brown eyes with slight hood, straight black hair to shoulders with soft waves at ends, slim athletic build. Tailored cream silk blouse with subtle texture, high neck, three quarter sleeves, small pearl button at collar. Dark charcoal slim trousers. Minimal gold hoop earrings. Soft diffused daylight from left, gentle rim light on hair, natural skin tones.

**Reference image prompt used to create the bible image:**

"photoreal, commercial beauty lighting, South Asian woman mid 30s warm brown skin sharp jaw deep brown eyes straight black shoulder length hair with soft waves, cream silk high neck blouse, charcoal trousers, soft left light, clean background, 8k detail"

**How it was used in the campaign:**

- 5 shots for the 30s hero video.
- Style block pasted verbatim into Kling and Veo prompts.
- One reference image generated once, reused.
- Action blocks swapped: "applies cream to cheek while looking at camera", "turns to show product on shelf", "smiles softly while speaking benefits", etc.
- Result: face and clothing stayed consistent enough that the client approved without post fixes on identity.

**Before this system:**

I was regenerating 4 out of 5 clips. Face would be a different person by shot 3. Client noticed.

**After:**

One regeneration per sequence at most. The locked bible + reference became the single source of truth.
